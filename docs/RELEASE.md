# Move Bitwig 1.0

Make music in Bitwig Studio with Ableton Move: choose a sound, play pads, build a phrase, and launch your song.

- Session launchers for seven tracks and four scenes, with scene launch and stop.
- Melodic pads, a 16-step sequencer that follows the selected track, and a graphical phrase overview.
- Sixteen drum pads and sixteen expression modifiers for velocity, chance, repeats, pan, and more.
- Device presets, FX browsing, remote parameters, and contextual feedback on Move and in Bitwig.
- Mixer access with Shift + Menu and a choice of Main volume or Bitwig's last-touched parameter on the Volume knob.

Requires **Bitwig Studio 6**, **Ableton Move with Schwung**, and a USB-C data connection.

Download **both** assets: install `move-bitwig-module.tar.gz` on Move with Schwung Manager, and extract all files from `move-bitwig-controller-scripts.zip` into your Bitwig Controller Scripts/Move folder. Choose **Ableton Move (Standalone Port)** for input and output in Bitwig.

See the [setup guide](https://github.com/pi43r/move-bitwig/blob/v1.0.0/README.md) and [control reference](https://github.com/pi43r/move-bitwig/blob/v1.0.0/docs/CONTROLS.md), including the macOS fix if the Standalone Port is missing.
